2. The App

Revealix is a casual word-puzzle game for iOS where you reveal a hidden target word by removing decoy letters. The App offers:

  • 10 themed regions × 100 levels of campaign content
  • Endless Zen mode and a daily challenge
  • Themed word packs and seasonal events
  • A 3-star scoring system, a no-hint streak, and a global score
  • The Book of Unveiling — a 50-verse wisdom manuscript hidden across the campaign
  • 5 boosters (Reveal, Scout, Skip, Reshuffle, First Letter)
  • 4 user-interface languages: English, French, Spanish, German
  • Optional Game Center leaderboards
